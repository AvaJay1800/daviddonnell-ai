I spent eight years designing enterprise software for construction and field operations teams at companies like Trimble, where the tools either fit how people work or they don't get used. I know what good technology looks like from the inside, and I know what it looks like when it misses.

I've been in Denver since 2008 and I work almost exclusively with local businesses. I'd rather help a Front Range contractor free up ten hours a week than pitch remotely to clients I'll never meet.
